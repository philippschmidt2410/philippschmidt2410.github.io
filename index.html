Research

My work is empirical, in international trade, labour economics and applied econometrics.

WORK IN PROGRESS

One Shock, Two Europes: The (Asymmetric) Labor Market Effects of Chinese Import Competition

I estimate the effect of Chinese import competition on manufacturing employment across EU-15 regions, using a shift-share instrument in the Borusyak–Hull–Jaravel framework with exposure-robust inference. The aggregate effect is close to zero, but it conceals a sharp divergence between German regions and the rest of the EU-15.

Master's thesis, Toulouse School of Economics, 2026. Supervisor: Paul Diegert, PhD.
